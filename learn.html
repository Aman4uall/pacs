A few things
you might wonder.

    
Do I need coding experience?
It depends on the project and how far you want to take it. Tell us your starting point when you enquire so we can suggest a suitable learning plan.

    
Do I need to bring a laptop?
A laptop is useful for websites, reports and interactive lessons. Ask us about device requirements and availability for the project you choose.

    
Are AI tool subscriptions included?
Tool access and subscription costs depend on the project. Ask for these alongside the course fee before joining.

    
Can I work on my own business or idea?
Tell us what you have in mind. We’ll discuss a manageable project and what you need to bring before you start.

    
When does it start, and what does it cost?
Open a project and choose “Get dates & fees”. We’ll share the available dates, scope and fees for that project.
