    Questions parents ask

    Does my child need to know coding?

    No. The Introductory Programme needs no coding at all. The Creator Programme teaches Python from the very beginning.

    Won't AI just do their homework for them?

    Not here. The AI tools we set up give hints before answers, and every build has to be explained in the student's own words.

    How big are the batches?

    At most 12 students for every teacher, and one device for every student in every session.

    Which AI tools do they use? Is it safe?

    Tools like Teachable Machine, ChatGPT and Gemini learning modes, NotebookLM and Replit. Parents give written consent for every tool before the first session. Tools with an 18+ age limit are used only from teacher accounts, and no personal photos or details are ever uploaded.

    What about school exams?

    Creator batches pause for school exam weeks, and Class 10 and 12 batches finish before pre-board exams. The courses support CBSE AI (Code 417), CBSE Computational Thinking and AI, and ICSE Robotics and AI.

    Will this improve their marks?

    They build the habits behind better marks: study routines, practice from their own syllabus, and explanations that finally click. You'll see the growth yourself in a skill check at the start and the end.

    Message us on WhatsApp and we'll share fees and dates for the next batch.
