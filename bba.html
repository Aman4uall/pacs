Questions students ask

Will this get me a job?

It gives you what interviewers ask to see: real work samples, a client project, and interview practice built on your own work.

Do I need to know coding?

No. Everything is done with AI tools and spreadsheets.

Does it replace CAT or CET coaching?

No. The Studio's MBA-ready track helps with your profile, your "why MBA" story and your interviews.

When do batches run?

Weekends and evenings, planned around university semester exams. Nothing runs during exams.
