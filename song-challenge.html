Rules and prize

Read the full Terms and Conditions
Who can enter?

Only students currently in Classes 8–12, including PUC students, from any school or board. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to the terms.

How are songs judged?

By the PACS AI team, on the idea, the words and how well the style fits. Songs with the student's own words stand out.

What's the prize, and when are the results?

One in every 10 songs wins ₹500, so 50 songs means 5 winners. The PACS AI team picks the best songs, and results are announced on this page.

Learn. Create. Put AI to work.

PACS AI is a hands-on AI lab in Mangalore, run by PACS Global. Class 8–12 and BBA students build apps, AI models and real client work; everyone else picks the AI skills they want and learns those.
