Step 2 · Make it

Turn it into a song on Suno.

Suno is an AI music website. You give it your words and a style, and it sings them back as a full song. The free plan is enough.

  1. Sign up at suno.com

    Create a free account. The free plan gives you about 10 songs a day.

  2. Open Create, then Custom

    Custom mode lets you use your own words instead of letting the AI write them.

  3. Paste your lyrics

    Use your starter from Step 1, with every ✎ line replaced. Keep the [Verse] and [Chorus] labels: they tell Suno how to shape the song.

  4. Add the style and a title

    The style tells Suno how the song should sound. Don't use real singers' names.

  5. Press Create and listen

    You get two versions. Change a word or the style and try again. Great songs usually take a few tries.

  6. Copy your song's link

    In your Library, find your best version and tap Copy link (or ⋯ then Share). Leave it on "Link only". It doesn't need to be public.

Rules, prize and privacy

Read the full Terms and Conditions
Who can enter?

Only students currently in Classes 8–12, including PUC students, from any school or board. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to the terms.

Does it cost anything?

No. Entry is free, and Suno's free plan is enough to make your song.

How many songs can I send?

One song per person. If you make a better version later, send it again and we'll judge your latest entry.

How are songs judged?

By the PACS AI team, on three things: the idea, the words, and how well the style fits the song. Songs where students wrote the words themselves stand out.

What's the prize, and when are the results?

One in every 10 songs wins ₹500, so the more songs come in, the more winners there are: 50 songs means 5 winners. The winners are the best songs, picked by the PACS AI team. Results are announced on this page.

Which AI music websites can I use?

Any, as long as you follow its rules, including its age rules, and can send us a link. We show Suno because it's free and easy to use.

What happens to our details?

Only the PACS AI team sees them, and we use them only to run this challenge. We never sell them. The Terms and Conditions explain exactly what we keep and for how long.
