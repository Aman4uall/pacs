A few practical questions

Before we
begin.

Is this for teachers of every subject?

Yes. Maths, science, languages, social science, even art and PE. Every exercise starts from the teacher’s own subject and class.

Do teachers need to know how to code?

No. Nothing in the training needs code. If a teacher can use WhatsApp, they can do this.

Does it count towards CPD hours?

Yes. CBSE counts a half-day expert-led session in school as 3 CPD hours, and a full day as 6 (Notification TRG-02, April 2026). We give you the attendance and topic record.

Will every teacher build what is shown here?

In the 8-hour option, every teacher leaves with an interactive lesson, classroom resources and their own Custom AI. The 4-hour option covers using AI in lessons, assessment and copying.

Where does it happen?

At your school, or at our office in Mangalore. Most schools prefer their own lab, so teachers practise on the machines they teach with.

What does it cost?

It depends on the number of teachers and the option you choose. Message us on WhatsApp and we send a quote.
