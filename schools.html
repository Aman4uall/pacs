    Questions
    What schools ask first.
    
      
    
        
    Does it count towards CPD hours?
    CBSE counts a half-day expert-led session in school as 3 hours of school-based CPD, and a full day as 6, under Domain II (Notification TRG-02). Your school records the hours; we give you the attendance and topic record.
    
        
    Do our teachers need a computer background?
    No. Nothing needs code. If they can teach, they can do this. It is built for every subject teacher, not only computer teachers.
    
        
    4 hours or 8?
    The 4-hour core fits a half-day and covers the essentials. The full 8 hours adds the no-code website and the Custom AI. Either can be split across days.
    
        
    Your campus or ours?
    Either. Most schools prefer their own lab, so teachers practise on the machines they will teach on.
    
        
    Are you replacing our teachers?
    No. Your teachers teach the subject and set the paper. We train them and hand over the material.
